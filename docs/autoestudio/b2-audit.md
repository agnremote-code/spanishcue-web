Explicit source mediation has been added at week 5. Unsupported claims that browser synthesis demonstrates Chilean or Rioplatense phonetics have been revised to require real identified examples in class.

## Four skills and transversal pronunciation

- **Reading:** 6,882 words of original input. Normal weeks: 302–321 words, with distinct articles, correspondence and narrative. Checkpoints: 462–496 words across positions and supplemental sources. Tasks include inference, evidence, limits, audience adaptation and interpretation, alongside selected responses.
- **Listening:** 5,313 words in separate original interaction scripts. Normal weeks: 229–265 words; checkpoints: 333–378. Each has multiple named interlocutors, global listening, detail listening and interpretive noticing. The shared engine owns transcript hiding; scripts are not inserted as reading passages.
- **Writing:** 220–280 words per ordinary task; final checkpoint 250–300. Planning, full models within the assigned word range, register, cohesion, source limits, production and revision are required. Grammar transformations and guided fragments lead into the independent output.
- **Speaking:** a 180-second presentation plus a 240-second interaction/mediation task each week. Roles, follow-up challenges, evidence, conditions, reformulation and live-class continuation are explicit.
- **Pronunciation:** requests and mitigation; tense stress; compound verbal groups; reproach; concession; relative-clause grouping; conditional focus; news rhythm; citation; exposition; conjecture; clitic chains; derived-word stress; conversational markers; narrative rhythm and voseo. Perception clips compare real competing forms. Production and interpretation are self/teacher reviewed.

The browser's synthesis engine supplies playback. Locale tags are preferences, not verified native accents or distinct recorded people. No new recorded assets are claimed. Chilean /s/, /ch/, `po`, and Rioplatense sheísmo are described and assigned comparison with a real identified sample in class; synthesis is explicitly not evidence of those features. No audio has been claimed as human-listened, pronunciation-scored or uploaded. The four macro-skills are preserved; pronunciation is not a fifth examination paper.

## Reformulation grading review

Independent review identified a false negative in week 11: an instruction requesting a formal equivalent of `por eso` accepted only `por consiguiente`, despite valid alternatives such as `por tanto`. A second instruction allowed multiple affirmed concessions but accepted only one sentence beginning with `aunque`.

The subsequent audit covered all 80 B2 reformulations (60 grammar tasks and 20 quiz tasks). Word order, reporting perspective, lexical alternatives and equivalent subjunctive forms make an exhaustive exact-answer list inappropriate. These tasks now use open production with the source text, original communicative instruction, an illustrative model and a checklist explicitly allowing other valid formulations. They are not automatically graded by text coincidence. Controlled gaps, corrections, ordering, matching, listening and selected responses retain their existing feedback. All modules still pass the unchanged validator and mixed-assessment requirements.

Regression coverage now includes this open-production policy: `node --test tests/autoestudio-b2.test.mjs` reports **4 passed, 0 failed**.


## Full writing-model review

All twenty writing models now demonstrate complete responses within their assigned ranges, with coherent paragraphs, the requested genre, source attribution or narrative perspective, register and a developed conclusion. Ordinary/checkpoint models contain 233–257 words against their 220–280-word tasks; the final model contains 278 words against 250–300. The earlier partial-model framing has been removed from the writing context. Short examples in practice and speaking remain deliberately separate scaffolds.

A regression test checks every model against its own task range, requires paragraph organization and rejects partial-model framing. `node --test tests/autoestudio-b2.test.mjs`: **5 passed, 0 failed** after this review.
