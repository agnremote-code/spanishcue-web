# B1 editorial and coverage audit

Audit date: 2026-10-02. Scope: the 20 B1 modules, B1 objective map and isolated B1 tests. The existing thirteen-station A1 data contract remains unchanged. Every week is a static `Module`; no runtime content factory or A1 sentence expansion is used.

## Sources and interpretation

Official sources consulted before drafting and revisited during the nominal-system audit:

- Council of Europe, [CEFR Companion Volume (2020)](https://rm.coe.int/common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching/16809ea0d4): B1 oral/written reception, connected production, interaction, mediation and phonological control scales. The practical design uses connected accounts, reasons for decisions, checking understanding and relaying relevant information. Intelligibility is the target; an accent is not a defect.
- Instituto Cervantes, [PCIC grammar, B1–B2 inventory](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/02_gramatica_inventario_b1-b2.htm): sections 1–7 for nominal/reference systems and pronouns, 9 for verbs, and 14–15 for discourse and subordination. This inventory separates B1 and B2 columns; the shared page is not evidence that every listed construction is B1. The retained hypothetical imperfect-subjunctive pattern and past-probability conditional are a bounded transition to B2, not an assertion that the PCIC classifies them as B1 requirements.
- Instituto Cervantes, [PCIC functions, B1–B2](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/05_funciones_inventario_b1-b2.htm): giving and requesting information, opinions, emotional reactions, influence and social interaction. These inform situations involving decisions, advice, requirements, clarification and complaints.
- Instituto Cervantes, [PCIC pronunciation and prosody, B1–B2](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/03_pronunciacion_inventario_b1-b2.htm): sound, stress, rhythm, intonation and meaningful grouping. Perception precedes production, and live interaction checks interpretation.
- Instituto Cervantes, [DELE B1 examination guide](https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/guia_examen_dele_b1.pdf) and [official B1 overview](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/examenes/b1): the four skills, sustained oral turns followed by interaction, practical text interpretation and purposeful written tasks informed the course tasks. No examination passage or item was reproduced. Module timings and word ranges are course-design choices, not official examination specifications.

This is SpanishCue original learning material, not official Cervantes material, an official DELE course, or a certification of proficiency. The objective map records teachable outcomes; it does not claim that completing one finite set of texts teaches every lexical item in the PCIC.

## Objective audit and revisions

- 120 objective IDs: all introduced exactly once in their planned week. The 112 inherited IDs remain available for downstream prerequisites; eight additions close gaps in the original verb-heavy map: nominal reference, adjective degree, discourse demonstratives, possessives, quantification/negation, combined clitics, reciprocal/reflexive se and process periphrases.
- Original 22-week plan compressed to twenty without deleting objectives. Original probability week joins interview week 9; checkpoints are 5/10/15/20. The old final preposition/relationship unit is distributed across weeks 2/7/12/16/19.
- Every objective introduced before week 20 has a later retrieval. Core weeks retrieve the previous week; checkpoints revisit the preceding block. Added grammar outcomes have explicit later tasks and are used in input and production instructions. There are 202 declared review references, including the initial A2 retrieval.
- Final-week new outcomes are integrative review/writing outcomes, avoiding an unreviewed new grammar target at the end.
- Corrected the inherited claim that `me alegra verte` is explained simply by grammatical subject identity. Experiencer, embedded action and full construction are distinguished.
- Replaced unsupported regional-perception promises in the retained `r-andina`, `j-variacion` and `costumbres-variedades` IDs. The outcomes now describe intelligibility, repair and contextual comprehension; no synthetic voice is offered as evidence of a regional realization.
- Reframed the old mystery inference target around evidence in a personal account and corrected the unsupported `ta` reduction expectation. Stable IDs are historical identifiers; displayed topics/outcomes describe what is actually taught.

## Skill and content evidence

- Reading: 20 original texts, 201–254 words each; 4,553 words total. Narratives, chronicles, practical articles and decision dossiers require explicit comprehension plus a short mediated summary. Two selected passages support language noticing.
- Listening: 20 distinct six-turn original conversations/interviews, 206–259 words each; 4,588 words total. All use gist → detail → noticing. Each stage contains two situation-specific questions. The shared engine controls transcript reveal; the data never asks the learner to read the transcript before the first listen.
- Writing: twenty distinct recipient/purpose tasks, 140–220-word ranges; planning, short drafting, original full models (149–197 words), language resources, checklist and revision. Every model falls within its task’s production range and demonstrates paragraph organization; these are original course examples, not official exam answers.
- Speaking: at least two substantive tasks every week. A 120-second organized turn (180 at checkpoints) is followed by a 120-second interaction that requires a question, response and reformulation. The classroom continuation introduces a new constraint.
- Pronunciation: weekly perception with audio, three or more production prompts, and targeted guidance on narrative groups, stress, reductions, linking, emphasis, quotation boundaries, sound clarity, conditional/argument grouping and data confirmation. Emotion and pragmatic prosody are explored with a real interlocutor rather than treated as a verified property of synthetic audio.
- 268 exercise blocks and 160 mixed quiz items. Quizzes combine comprehension, listening, gap completion, a guided reformulation with model/self-check, a distinct error repair, ordering and meaningful short production. Correct option positions vary. Category tasks distinguish communicative or grammatical functions, not merely a word from its definition.
- Every module has classroom transfer and retrieval. Checkpoint 15 and final checkpoint require integrating different written/oral information rather than repeating an isolated quiz.

## Narrow assessment audit

The twenty quiz reformulations allow natural equivalent word orders and paraphrases. They now use the existing open-production mechanic with a model and explicit self-check rather than exact-string automatic marking. Controlled gap completion, ordering and error correction remain automatically checked. Source-dependent retrieval supplies case facts, quoted messages or paired written/oral updates directly in the task; learners do not have to invent an absent source. A regression test checks both properties.

Full-model follow-up: all twenty writing models were expanded with situation-specific reasons, evidence, practical detail or limitations. All now meet their displayed word ranges (3,311 words total); a regression test checks range and paragraph organization.
