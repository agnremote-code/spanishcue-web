# A2 curriculum audit

Authored and checked 2026-10-02. This is original SpanishCue learning material informed by official standards. It is not an official Cervantes course, a reproduced DELE examination, or a certification of proficiency.

## Sources consulted before authoring

- Council of Europe, **CEFR Companion volume (2020)**, activity scales for listening, reading, oral/written production, interaction, transmitting specific information, and phonological control: https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4 ; publication portal: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-companion-volume-and-its-language-versions . A2 tasks centre on understandable, predictable everyday information, short connected messages, cooperative clarification and practical information transfer. Pronunciation targets intelligibility and repair, not accent removal.
- Instituto Cervantes, **PCIC, Gramática A1–A2**, particularly personal pronouns, possessives, quantifiers, past forms, affirmative imperatives, nonfinite forms and simple linking: https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/02_gramatica_inventario_a1-a2.htm . The A1 foundation is preserved rather than unnecessarily reintroduced. The A2 audit explicitly added spelling-preserving preterite forms, **ver/dar**, and tonic possessives to the inherited map.
- Instituto Cervantes, **PCIC, Funciones A1–A2**: https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/05_funciones_inventario_a1-a2.htm . Information exchange, requests, preferences, agreement, practical instructions and clarification inform the weekly transactions.
- Instituto Cervantes, **PCIC, Pronunciación y prosodia A1–A2**: https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/03_pronunciacion_inventario_a1-a2.htm . Perception precedes production. Stress, grouping, linking and pragmatic intonation recur in speaking as well as dedicated practice.
- Instituto Cervantes, **PCIC, Nociones específicas A1–A2**: https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/09_nociones_especificas_inventario_a1-a2.htm . Housing, work/study, health, shopping, services, travel and leisure drive the contexts. Housing/service comparisons received an explicit additional objective.
- Instituto Cervantes, **Guía del examen DELE A2, versión 2020**, reading pp. 8–9, listening pp. 10–11, writing pp. 12–13, oral tasks and assessment in the subsequent sections: https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/DELE_A2_v2020_Gu%C3%ADa_de_examen_0.pdf ; current official examination page: https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/examenes/a2 . Original task patterns practise selecting practical information, matching descriptions, understanding clear conversations/notices, writing to a recipient, and sustaining a prepared turn followed by interaction. Final checkpoint input builds stamina for longer texts; weekly work is not a replica of examination timing or paper layout.

No exam text, answer set or recorded exam audio was copied. The official DELE guide's acceptance of consistently used established Hispanic norms informs the inclusive treatment of varieties; synthetic voice language tags are not evidence of an accent.

## Scope decisions

The existing A1 teaching order, 13-section contract, data-only modules, local production tools and shared renderer are preserved. There are 20 A2 modules, with checkpoints at 5, 10, 15 and 20. Each checkpoint combines information from different sources with an actual writing and interaction outcome. Learners receive Spanish explanations and selected English support/glosses, not a parallel English course.

A2 is an everyday communication level, not a promise of unrestricted independence. The inherited sequence was retained, including modest guided extensions in future simple, real conditions, and common conditional advice/courtesy chunks. These are **curriculum progression choices**, not a claim that every such form is an A2 PCIC minimum. Future planning remains anchored in familiar contexts. Productive subjunctive and pluperfect mastery are not assessed at this level; an occasional service formula can be understood as a chunk.

Five objective gaps were made explicit: `a2.gram.indefinido-ortografia`, `a2.gram.indefinido-ver-dar`, `a2.gram.posesivos-tonicos`, `a2.voc.vivienda-servicios`, and `a2.fun.transmitir-acuerdo`. Each has new teaching, later retrieval and prerequisites. The original `a2.pron.s-aspirada` ID is stable, but its outcome now accurately asks for awareness of variation, contextual interpretation and repetition, without pretending that synthetic audio demonstrates verified Caribbean speech.

## Progression and evidence

| Weeks | Communication | Systems and phonology | Integrated result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4 | Experiences, completed days, biographies, childhood | Perfect → regular/irregular preterite → imperfect; linking, verbal stress, written accents, variation/repair | Personal messages and interviews with meaningful time reference |
| 5 | Family memory exhibition | Retrieve the first four weeks | Cartelas, source checking and a visitor interview |
| 6–9 | An incident, housing choice, loans, instructions | Narrative contrast, comparison, object combinations, affirmative commands; grouping, contrastive focus, attached-pronoun stress | Tell a problem, choose a room, negotiate a favour and explain a recipe |
| 10 | Neighbourhood outing | Retrieve narration, comparisons, pronouns and instructions | Choose between destinations, allocate equipment and communicate a plan |
| 11–14 | Changing routines, practical plans, appointments, service problems | Aspectual periphrases, guided future/condition patterns, symptom language, courtesy; nasal linking, vowels, x, intonation | Study update, backup plan, appointment and reservation complaint |
| 15 | Disrupted journey | Retrieve the preceding four weeks and transmit an agreement | Reconcile transport, accommodation and activity deadlines by phone and in writing |
| 16–19 | Study/work decisions, deliveries, lost property, local opinions | Causal/temporal links, por/para, indefinites, relatives, tonic possessives, opinion/turn-taking; rhythm and pragmatic prosody | Connected explanations, a delivery solution, description/identification and reasoned disagreement |
| 20 | Neighbourhood book exchange | Retrieve weeks 16–19 and integrate earlier functions | A source-based proposal, responsibilities, pending questions and negotiated change |

Every week has reading, listening, writing and speaking. The objective domain counts below describe the source-of-truth map, not separate lesson totals; grammar and vocabulary feed all four skills. Pronunciation is transversal and is never presented as a fifth exam paper.

- **Reading:** 20 original texts/dossiers, 140–404 words each; 3,704 words total. Genres include messages, diary, biography, recollection, exhibit captions, narrative, room adverts, recipe, blog, diary of learning, programme, clinic information, booking terms, travel notices, testimony, tracking/correspondence, lost-property board, forum and final dossier. Each has different-mechanic comprehension plus noticing; housing and lost-property tasks additionally classify details across notices.
- **Listening:** 20 distinct scripts, 114–307 words each; 3,091 words total. Every module uses gist → detail → noticing, with optional transcript controlled by the shared engine. The input is distinct from its reading. Later checkpoints expand to sustained exchanges and multiple practical constraints.
- **Writing:** 60–100 words initially, then 80–120, later 100–140, and 120–150 in the final checkpoint. Every complete model falls inside the requested range. Planning, useful language, explicit revision and a checklist accompany actual production.
- **Speaking:** two substantive tasks weekly; 180–240 seconds total per module, 66 minutes of scheduled production across the level before repetition/class transfer. Prepared turns combine with follow-up questions, clarification, changed constraints and negotiated outcomes.
- **Review:** two-week retrieval in ordinary weeks, block retrieval at checkpoints, and additional retrieval of prior checkpoint outcomes. All 107 objectives introduced before week 20 return through identifiable production activities. The two terminal week-20 objectives have no later A2 module; they consolidate the whole level rather than being falsely marked as later-reviewed.

`app/autoestudio/curriculum/modules/a2/coverage.json` is the machine-readable companion, with every objective's introduction, subsequent review modules and exact retrieval exercise IDs. `a2RetrievalEvidence` connects the declarations to real open tasks with models/checklists. Review missions require the target forms, vocabulary, sound feature and communicative function in a new example; they are not merely lists of objective IDs.

## Validation and limits

- All 20 literal `Module` values pass `validateModule` with zero problems.
- All 109 mapped objectives are introduced exactly once in the declared week; no orphan references or missing mapped objectives.
- No accidental repeated reading, listening, exercise or quiz item under the existing whole-course duplication audit.
- Six A2 tests exercise structural completeness, objective-to-retrieval evidence, input/production substance, model lengths/checkpoint progression and source-gap additions/audio honesty, and explicitly permitted exact-check variants.
- The canonical 17-test Autoestudio suite also passes alongside the A2 tests. TypeScript and scoped ESLint checks pass.
- This is curricular coverage of the declared and audited map. Automated presence checks do not alone prove learning, official equivalence or human assessment quality. Oral output is self-/teacher-reviewed; no automatic accent or quality score is claimed.
- No finite recorded assets were created by this content change. Existing engine playback may use browser synthesis. No human listening, verified regional voice, studio recording, audio upload or speaker-quality evaluation is claimed. Auth/access, responsive browser QA and deployment remain integration responsibilities outside these owned content files.

## Final exact-answer audit

All 60 A2 transformation items were rechecked against the engine's full-string comparison. Instructions now constrain the target form, retained words and clause order; subject inclusion is specified or both alternatives are accepted. Common optional-subject variants and the longer disagreement response have explicit accepted answers. The broader communicative reformulations remain open tasks with models and checklists. Retrieval activities include a solo two-role procedure and do not claim absent acoustic or regional evidence. The targeted variant regression test exercises the actual answer checker.
