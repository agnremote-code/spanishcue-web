## Queue policy and scope

The first wave rotates **Gramática → Fonética → Escucha → Vocabulario**. Priority favors demonstrable learner-facing defects, missing oral mechanisms, repairable strong foundations and curricular use cases. It does not rank by alphabet or create mechanical six-level copies.

**20 tasks: five in each category.** Several explicitly bounded repair packages cover two or three existing records. Together the tasks target **23 existing lessons plus 10 proposed new lessons (33 lesson targets)**. They are scheduled in **15 future runs of 2–3 lesson targets**. A queue task is not necessarily one lesson. NEW count means proposed work, not created inventory.

Conversation Batch 1 stays intact. The four other categories take this production cycle; any later Conversation batch requires separate prioritization. Core asset/route/content preservation remains in force until a future brief explicitly authorizes the specific repair.
