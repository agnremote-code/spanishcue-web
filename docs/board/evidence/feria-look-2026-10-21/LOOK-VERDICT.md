## Martin: please AVOID these as distinguishing features right now
Naming them in the text is fine where the checker allows it. Just never let them be what rules a distractor out.

1. **body (f/m)**: illegible. With the same hair and clothes, f and m look identical at 8 m.
2. **age young / adult / old**: illegible. Only **child** reads, by size. Old only reads through `hairColor:canoso`.
3. **pelo largo**: illegible from the front. The hair falls behind the head, so it reads as pelo corto.
4. **coleta, moño, pelo rizado, trenzas**: illegible. They all look like short hair from the front.
5. **calvo/a**: marginal. It only works with light skin; on dark skin it reads as short dark hair.
6. **gorra**: marginal. The cap colour is random, and a dark cap disappears.
7. **mochila (Tier A!)**: marginal. From the front only the straps show.
8. **gafas, bufanda**: illegible. **barba**: only on light skin.
9. **alto/bajo**: marginal or illegible. The slot depth offsets (±0.5 m) cancel the 0.14 m gap.
10. **sentado/a**: marginal. There's no seat, so it reads as a crouch or a shorter person.
11. **Bottom colours marrón, morado, negro**: marginal or illegible. The legs are thin and dark, and brown legs read as skin or black.
12. **vestido vs abrigo**: they look alike (the coat is knee-length). **camiseta vs any long-sleeved top**: marginal, because the forearms are skin-coloured and on dark skin they read as sleeves.
13. **paraguas, flores, mapa** (held): small. They show, but are marginal.

**Safe (legible) to lean on:**
- **Top colours**: rojo, naranja, amarillo, rosa, azul, verde, blanco, negro, gris.
- **Hair colours**: rubio, pelirrojo, canoso, moreno.
- **Tops and bottoms**: vestido and delantal vs camiseta; falda vs pantalón.
- **Poses**: tocando la guitarra, saludando, señalando, llevando una caja, leyendo.
- **Holds**: maleta, which is OK; it's dark on dark ground.

**Layout issues that affect every set** (Sofia's side, not content):
- The mascot hides the lower body of slot 2 (x −0.8).
- On a phone in portrait, slot 4 (x +2.4) is half outside the frame.
- At informacion, a big blue wedge (the stall awning) crosses the lower third of the frame.
