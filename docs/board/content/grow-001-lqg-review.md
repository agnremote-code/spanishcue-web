This unit is 1 circle (4 beats + 5 questions) plus 1 listening and 10 role cards. That's less interaction volume, but the listening and the cards are new **kinds** of free asset. I read N3 as a volume and PRO-content rule (no PRO content moves), so I consider it satisfied. I'd like your explicit view.
