# SpanishCue language policy

- Default Spanish is contemporary, internationally understandable Spanish. Address one learner with **tú**, including standard imperatives and clitic accents: escucha, elige, dilo, cuéntanos, ayúdanos.
- Complete general conjugation paradigms include **yo; tú; él/ella/usted; nosotros/nosotras; vosotros/vosotras; ellos/ellas/ustedes**. Usted and ustedes take third-person verb forms. Do not turn the product into Spain-only Spanish.
- Preserve regional language only when pedagogically intentional: voseo lessons/comparisons, explicitly Argentine vocabulary/phonetics, authentic regional dialogue and quotations. A setting in Argentina does not exempt surrounding instructions, teacher notes or navigation.
- Keep recorded audio, scripts, transcripts and word timings synchronized. Edit independent UI freely; regenerate an unintended regional spoken model through the canonical audio pipeline before changing its transcript. Document unavailable generation precisely.
- The language audit changes Spanish only. Preserve English copy, including translated examples and explanation objects.
- Run the neutral-Spanish guard and English-copy comparison. Record precise reviewed exceptions with exact path, text and reason in the audit registry. Do not exempt an entire lesson to hide ordinary UI errors. Standard first-person preterites and imperative da + le require contextual review, not mechanical replacement.
