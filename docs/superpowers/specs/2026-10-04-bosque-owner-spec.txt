==================================================
CORE IDEA
==================================================

EL BOSQUE DE LOS HONGOS GIGANTES

The learner controls the OFFICIAL SpanishCue mascot as an original superhero inside an enormous realistic-fantasy forest.

It is a THIRD-PERSON 3D PLATFORMER.

The learner:

- runs;
- walks;
- jumps;
- climbs through routes;
- lands on giant mushroom caps;
- crosses logs;
- uses vines / wooden bridges where appropriate;
- enters caves;
- reaches high branches;
- bounces from selected mushroom caps;
- discovers hidden paths;
- finds optional conversation zones;
- reaches a huge final mushroom at the top of the forest.

BUT THE GAMEPLAY IS THE CONTAINER.

THE ACTUAL PEDAGOGY IS PURE CONVERSATION.

DEPENDE

9. FUTURO

10. CAMBIA DE IDEA

11. DEFIENDE LO CONTRARIO

choose an opinion, then briefly defend the opposite

12. GRAN HONGO FINAL
mixed conversation challenge.
